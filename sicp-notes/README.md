# SICP Notes

My notes and flashcards from working through [Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs](https://mitp-content-server.mit.edu/books/content/sectbyfn/books_pres_0/6515/sicp.zip/index.html) (Abelson & Sussman), covering Chapter 1: Building Abstractions with Procedures.

## What's here

- **[notes.md](./notes.md)** - Reading notes covering Sections 1.0 through 1.2, including the Counting Change problem. Covers expressions, evaluation models, the substitution model, compound procedures, recursive vs iterative processes, and tree recursion.
- **[flashcards.md](./flashcards.md)** - 80+ Q&A flashcards in spaced repetition format, covering everything from primitive expressions and special forms through to the mechanics of tree-recursive space complexity.

## Topics covered

- **1.0 Introduction** - What are processes, procedures, and programs? McCarthy's 1960 Lisp paper.
- **1.1 The Elements of Programming** - Expressions, naming, environments, the substitution model, applicative vs normal order evaluation, special forms, compound procedures, lexical scoping.
- **1.2 Procedures and the Processes They Generate** - Linear recursive vs linear iterative processes, the connection between head/tail recursion and SICP's recursive/iterative process terminology, tree recursion, time and space complexity of tree-recursive processes, Fibonacci both ways.
- **Programming Paradigms** - Imperative vs declarative, procedural programming, etymology of "statement" and "state".
