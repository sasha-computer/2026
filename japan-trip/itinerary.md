# Japan Trip Itinerary - November 2026

**Dates**: November 10-27, 2026 (17 full days + travel)
**Route**: Tokyo → Kanazawa → Takayama → Kyoto → Hiroshima → Tokyo

---

## Pre-Trip Checklist
- [ ] Book flights (London → Tokyo Narita/Haneda, return from Tokyo)
- [ ] Purchase 14-day JR Pass
- [ ] Book accommodation (especially ryokans - book 3+ months ahead)
- [ ] Download offline maps (Google Maps Japan, or Maps.me)
- [ ] Get Suica/Pasmo card on arrival (or Apple Pay Suica)
- [ ] Travel insurance
- [ ] Check visa requirements (UK: visa-free for 90 days)

---

## Day 1 (Nov 10) - Arrival in Tokyo
**Flight**: London → Tokyo (arrive late afternoon)

- Clear immigration, collect luggage
- Get Suica card at airport station
- Train to Yanaka/Nezu area (via Skyliner to Ueno, walk or metro to hotel)
- Light dinner near hotel, early night (jet lag)

**Stay**: Yanaka/Nezu area

---

## Day 2 (Nov 11) - Tokyo: Old Town
**Area focus**: Yanaka, Nezu, Sendagi (Yanesen)

Morning:
- Yanaka Cemetery walk (historic, peaceful, cats)
- Yanaka Ginza shopping street (traditional snacks, local shops)

Afternoon:
- Nezu Shrine (stunning vermillion torii gates, azalea gardens)
- Explore backstreets of Yanaka (temples, old wooden houses)

Evening:
- Coffee in the area
- Dinner: Izakaya in Yanaka or Sendagi

**Stay**: Yanaka/Nezu

---

## Day 3 (Nov 12) - Tokyo: Museums & Coffee
**Area focus**: Ueno, Kiyosumi-Shirakawa

Morning:
- Ueno Park (Tokyo National Museum or National Museum of Western Art)
- Ameyoko market (under the train tracks)

Afternoon:
- Metro to Kiyosumi-Shirakawa (coffee district)
- Blue Bottle Coffee (their first Japan location)
- Explore specialty coffee scene
- Kiyosumi Garden (traditional landscape garden)

Evening:
- Dinner in Monzen-Nakacho (old-town Edo vibes)

**Stay**: Yanaka/Nezu

---

## Day 4 (Nov 13) - Day Trip: Kamakura
**Train**: Tokyo → Kamakura (~1 hour, covered by JR Pass)

Morning:
- Great Buddha (Kotoku-in)
- Hasedera Temple (sea views, gardens)

Afternoon:
- Walk to Yuigahama Beach
- Komachi-dori shopping street
- Tsurugaoka Hachimangu shrine

Evening:
- Return to Tokyo
- Dinner in Ebisu (upscale residential, good restaurants)

**Stay**: Yanaka/Nezu

---

## Day 5 (Nov 14) - Tokyo: Creative Neighbourhoods
**Area focus**: Shimokitazawa, Daikanyama

Morning:
- Shimokitazawa (thrift shops, vintage clothing, record stores)
- Coffee at Bear Pond Espresso or similar

Afternoon:
- Daikanyama T-Site (stunning bookstore complex)
- Walk through Daikanyama residential streets (very Richmond-like)
- Optional: Nakameguro canal walk

Evening:
- Pack for Kanazawa
- Early night before travel day

**Stay**: Yanaka/Nezu

---

## Day 6 (Nov 15) - Tokyo → Kanazawa
**Train**: Tokyo → Kanazawa (Hokuriku Shinkansen, ~2.5 hours)

Morning:
- Final Tokyo morning, grab breakfast
- Train from Ueno or Tokyo Station

Afternoon:
- Arrive Kanazawa ~12:30
- Check into hotel
- Explore Higashi Chaya geisha district (traditional tea houses, gold leaf shops)

Evening:
- Walk along Asano River
- Dinner: Local seafood at Omicho Market area restaurant

**Stay**: Kazuemachi or Teramachi area

---

## Day 7 (Nov 16) - Kanazawa: Gardens & Samurai
Morning:
- Kenrokuen Garden early (one of Japan's top 3 gardens)
- Kanazawa Castle Park

Afternoon:
- 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art (free to enter main areas)
- Nagamachi samurai district (restored samurai houses, narrow lanes)
- Nomura-ke samurai house (beautifully preserved interior and garden)

Evening:
- Gold leaf experience/shopping
- Dinner: Kaiseki or local specialties

**Stay**: Kanazawa

---

## Day 8 (Nov 17) - Kanazawa: Markets & Temples
Morning:
- Omicho Market early (fresh seafood breakfast, local produce)
- Try kaisendon (sashimi rice bowl)

Afternoon:
- Myoryuji "Ninja Temple" (book ahead - hidden passages, trap doors)
- Teramachi temple district (quieter, residential)
- Kazuemachi Chaya district (smaller geisha district, atmospheric)

Evening:
- Sake tasting
- Final Kanazawa dinner

**Stay**: Kanazawa

---

## Day 9 (Nov 18) - Kanazawa → Shirakawa-go → Takayama
**Bus**: Kanazawa → Shirakawa-go → Takayama (not covered by JR Pass, ~¥5,000)

Morning:
- Early bus from Kanazawa (~9am)
- Arrive Shirakawa-go ~11am

Midday:
- Explore UNESCO village (2-3 hours)
- Gassho-zukuri thatched-roof houses
- Walk to observation point for village panorama
- Light lunch in village

Afternoon:
- Bus to Takayama (~1 hour)
- Arrive ~4pm
- Evening walk in Sanmachi Suji (historic merchant district)

Evening:
- Hida beef dinner (local specialty)
- Sake from local breweries

**Stay**: Takayama (consider ryokan with onsen)

---

## Day 10 (Nov 19) - Takayama
Morning:
- Morning markets (two to choose from):
  - Miyagawa Morning Market (along the river)
  - Jinya-mae Morning Market (near former government building)
- Fresh produce, pickles, crafts

Midday:
- Takayama Jinya (Edo-period government building)
- Hida Folk Village / Hida no Sato (open-air museum, traditional houses)

Afternoon:
- Sake brewery visits (several in Sanmachi Suji area)
- Sanmachi Suji exploring (shops, cafes, temples)

Evening:
- Hida beef in different preparation (skewers, sushi, etc.)
- Early night before travel day

**Stay**: Takayama

---

## Day 11 (Nov 20) - Takayama → Kyoto
**Train**: Takayama → Nagoya → Kyoto (~4 hours total)

Morning:
- Final Takayama morning
- Train via Nagoya (scenic route through mountains)

Afternoon:
- Arrive Kyoto ~2pm
- Check into hotel/machiya in Nishijin
- Evening walk to Gion (early evening is less crowded)
- Wander Hanamikoji street, Shirakawa canal

Evening:
- Dinner in Gion or Pontocho (narrow alley with restaurants)

**Stay**: Nishijin or Okazaki area

---

## Day 12 (Nov 21) - Kyoto: Arashiyama
**Strategy**: Go EARLY to beat crowds

Morning (7am start):
- Arashiyama Bamboo Grove (magical before 8am, crowded after 9am)
- Tenryu-ji Temple (Zen temple, beautiful garden)
- Okochi Sanso Villa (actor's estate, stunning views)

Midday:
- Walk along Katsura River
- Lunch in Arashiyama

Afternoon:
- Monkey Park Iwatayama (optional - hike up, views + monkeys)
- Return to central Kyoto
- Rest at hotel

Evening:
- Dinner in Nishijin neighbourhood (local izakayas)

**Stay**: Kyoto

---

## Day 13 (Nov 22) - Kyoto: Fushimi
**Strategy**: Early morning again for Fushimi Inari

Morning (6:30am start):
- Fushimi Inari Shrine (thousands of torii gates)
- Hike partway up Mt Inari (stunning at sunrise, empty)

Midday:
- Fushimi sake district
- Gekkeikan Okura Sake Museum
- Sake tasting at multiple breweries

Afternoon:
- Kizakura Kappa Country (sake + beer brewery with restaurant)
- Return to central Kyoto

Evening:
- Explore Nishiki Market (Kyoto's kitchen)
- Dinner near market

**Stay**: Kyoto

---

## Day 14 (Nov 23) - Kyoto: Philosopher's Path & Autumn Colours
**Note**: This is Labour Thanksgiving Day (holiday) - expect some crowds

Morning:
- Philosopher's Path walk (2km stone path along canal)
- Ginkaku-ji (Silver Pavilion) at north end
- Peak autumn colours should be stunning

Midday:
- Honen-in Temple (quieter, moss-covered)
- Lunch near Philosopher's Path

Afternoon:
- Nanzen-ji Temple (massive gate, aqueduct)
- Eikan-do Temple (famous for autumn colours - worth the entry fee)

Evening:
- Night illumination at temple (many temples do special autumn night viewing)

**Stay**: Kyoto

---

## Day 15 (Nov 24) - Day Trip: Nara or Uji
**Choose one** (or split the day):

### Option A: Nara
- Train: Kyoto → Nara (~45 min)
- Nara Park (friendly deer everywhere)
- Todai-ji Temple (world's largest wooden building, giant Buddha)
- Kasuga Taisha shrine (thousands of lanterns)
- Walk through Naramachi (old merchant district)

### Option B: Uji
- Train: Kyoto → Uji (~20 min)
- Byodo-in Temple (featured on ¥10 coin)
- Matcha everything (Uji is Japan's matcha capital)
- Nakamura Tokichi for matcha desserts
- Walk along Uji River

Evening:
- Return to Kyoto
- Final Kyoto dinner - splurge on nice kaiseki

**Stay**: Kyoto

---

## Day 16 (Nov 25) - Kyoto → Hiroshima
**Train**: Kyoto → Hiroshima (Sanyo Shinkansen, ~1.5 hours)

Morning:
- Pack and check out
- Any final Kyoto stops
- Train to Hiroshima

Afternoon:
- Arrive Hiroshima ~1pm
- Check into hotel
- Peace Memorial Park
- A-Bomb Dome (exterior)
- Peace Memorial Museum (allow 2+ hours, emotionally heavy)

Evening:
- Walk along river
- Okonomiyaki dinner (Hiroshima-style - layered, with noodles)
- Try Okonomi-mura or local restaurant

**Stay**: Hiroshima (Nagarekawa or Hondori area)

---

## Day 17 (Nov 26) - Miyajima Island
**Ferry**: Hiroshima → Miyajima (~50 min total including tram to ferry)

Morning:
- Early ferry to Miyajima
- Itsukushima Shrine (floating torii gate - iconic)
- Daisho-in Temple (atmospheric, hidden gem)

Midday:
- Mt Misen ropeway (views over Inland Sea)
- Optional: hike to summit from ropeway station
- Lunch on island (try momiji manju - maple leaf cakes)

Afternoon:
- Explore island at leisure
- Feed the deer
- Coffee/tea before heading back

Evening:
- Ferry back to Hiroshima
- Final Hiroshima dinner

**Stay**: Hiroshima

---

## Day 18 (Nov 27) - Hiroshima → Tokyo (Return)
**Train**: Hiroshima → Tokyo (Tokaido Shinkansen, ~4 hours)

Morning:
- Shukkeien Garden (if time)
- Or sleep in and relax

Midday:
- Shinkansen to Tokyo

Afternoon/Evening:
- Arrive Tokyo ~4-5pm
- Optional: Visit area not seen earlier (Shibuya crossing, Shinjuku, etc.)
- Or return to Yanaka for familiar comfort
- Final dinner

**Stay**: Near airport or central Tokyo

---

## Day 19 (Nov 28) - Departure
- Train to airport (Narita or Haneda)
- Flight home to London

## Accommodation Notes

### Ryokan Stays (book early!)
Recommended for 2-3 nights:
1. **Takayama** - Mountain town atmosphere, many options
2. **Miyajima** - Sleep on the island after day-trippers leave (magical)
3. **Kyoto outskirts** - Traditional machiya (townhouse) experience

### Budget per Night (per room, double occupancy)
- Budget: ¥8,000-12,000 (~£40-60)
- Mid-range: ¥15,000-25,000 (~£75-125)
- Nice ryokan: ¥30,000-50,000 (~£150-250)

## Packing Essentials
- [ ] Comfortable walking shoes (you'll walk 15-20km many days)
- [ ] Layers (November is 10-18°C, cooler in mountains)
- [ ] Light rain jacket
- [ ] Small day bag
- [ ] Portable battery pack
- [ ] Universal adapter (Japan: Type A, 100V)
- [ ] Cash (Japan is still quite cash-based outside cities)
